Changelog

Every release, newest first.

What changed in DivPic, from the first element screenshot to Figma mode. Dates are when each version was finished, so a store can show it a day or two later. A browser pill marks the version its store has right now — stores review at their own pace. Safari is coming.

  1. 5.1Oct 22, 2026Latest

    Figma layers you can edit Chrome

    • ImprovedFigma: far more frames paste with auto layout, and boxes hug their content or fill their parent instead of being fixed sizes.
    • ImprovedFigma: edit a button label or a paragraph and its frame grows with it.
    • ImprovedFigma: spacing goes over as padding, without the extra margin frames Figma used to add.
    • ImprovedFigma: full-page captures grab videos and 3D canvases below the fold as real frames, not placeholders.
    • ImprovedFigma: decorative highlights, glows, selected tabs, dividers and numbered list markers now paste.
    • FixedFigma: modern colors (P3, oklch) no longer paste blank or white, and filtered logos keep their color.
    • FixedFigma: collapsed FAQ boxes, centered rows and background canvases paste where the page shows them.
    • FixedIn fullscreen on Mac, the toolbar opens below Chrome's tabs instead of under them.
  2. 5.0Oct 20, 2026

    Inspect: read any page's design system

    • NewInspect: a new toolbar dock with Overview, Fonts, Colors, Images, DESIGN.md, Inspect element and Pick color.
    • NewInspect: Overview shows the page's colors, spacing scale, radius, shadows and page weight by resource type.
    • NewInspect: hover any element to see its box model and the distances to its neighbours, then click to lock it.
    • NewInspect: download the page's colors, fonts and images, one file at a time or as a zip.
    • NewInspect: turn the page into a DESIGN.md or SKILL.md for Claude Code, Cursor or Codex.
    • NewPress Enter to capture the highlighted element.
    • ImprovedFigma: carousels and sliders paste with their slides in place, cut to the part the page shows.
    • ImprovedFigma: flex and grid items keep the order you see on the page, even when CSS reorders them.
    • ImprovedFigma: canvases partly off screen keep their visible part, and shifted canvases stay where the page shows them.
    • FixedFigma: a picked element's outer margins no longer pad out the frame.
    • FixedFigma: pill labels no longer paste faded under their own background, and cards no longer jump up to the frame's top edge.
    • FixedFigma: footnote numbers paste small, and text around inline links and footnotes wraps and keeps its spaces like the page.
  3. 4.4Oct 18, 2026

    Figma exports that keep what's in front in front

    • ImprovedFigma: WebGL canvases, like animated hero scenes, come through as the frame on screen instead of blank.
    • ImprovedFigma: a video with no frame to show pastes as a video placeholder instead of an empty box.
    • ImprovedFigma: full-page export waits for fade-in effects to finish, so heroes are not captured half faded.
    • ImprovedFigma: headings with balanced wrapping keep the same line breaks as on the page.
    • FixedFigma: buttons and text no longer disappear behind a background image or video that the page draws underneath them.
    • FixedFigma: split, animated headlines keep the space between words, and hidden screen-reader text no longer shows up as a stray layer.
  4. 4.3Oct 18, 2026

    Full pages of any length, sharper Figma exports Firefox

    • NewFull-page PNG no longer trims the bottom of very tall pages. A page too tall for one image is saved as several PNGs (part-1, part-2, …) at full sharpness.
    • NewDelete your account from Settings, under Sign out.
    • ImprovedFigma: videos come through as their visible frame, including videos served from another site.
    • ImprovedFigma: rows of inline-block items, and flex rows with a background overlay in front, now paste as auto layout.
    • FixedFigma: picking an element covered by a decorative overlay captures the element itself, not the overlay.
    • FixedFigma: SVG icons whose shapes are set in CSS keep their geometry.
  5. 4.2.1Oct 17, 2026

    Clean element shots on busy pages

    • FixedCapturing an element on a page with a pinned or scroll-linked header, like github.com, no longer bakes strips of that header and other sticky bars into the PNG.
    • ImprovedAn element that fits on screen is scrolled fully into view and captured in one shot.
    • ImprovedThe toolbar comes back after every capture, cancel or error, and stays open until you close it.
    • ImprovedFirefox: the Firefox Add-ons version jumps from 3.2 to 4.2.1. On Firefox, usage stats are sent only if you opt in.
  6. 4.2Oct 16, 2026

    Whole-page PNGs and smarter picking

    • NewFull page PNG: DivPic scrolls the page, captures each screen and stitches them into one image, up to 32,000px tall.
    • NewA label over the highlighted element shows its tag, id, class and size before you click.
    • NewPress Arrow Up or Arrow Down to move to the parent or a child element before you click.
    • ImprovedPicking reaches into open shadow DOM.
    • ImprovedIn multi-select, picking a parent drops its children you already picked, and elements the page removes drop out too.
    • FixedAn element partly off screen is captured whole, instead of with a blank area where it overflowed.
  7. 4.1Jun 2, 2026

    A cleaner full-page shot

    • FixedThe toolbar hides itself before a full page PNG, so it no longer shows up in the screenshot.
    • NewAnonymous usage stats, such as which export formats get used. Nothing from the page you capture is sent. The privacy policy lists what is.
  8. 4.0May 31, 2026

    Figma mode and the in-page toolbar

    • NewFigma mode: copy an element or a whole page and paste it into Figma as editable frames with auto layout.
    • NewToolbar: a floating toolbar on the page replaces the popup. Click the DivPic icon to open it.
    • NewFull page capture as PNG or SVG.
    • NewSign in with Google to use Figma mode. PNG and SVG still need no account.
    • NewA "Contact the builder" button in the toolbar.
    • ImprovedSVG exports embed images, canvases and videos, including images from other sites.
    • ImprovedThe on-page badge tells you what to do next and has a Cancel button.
  9. 3.2Feb 17, 2026

    Back to the popup

    • ImprovedThe side panel from 3.1 is gone. DivPic works from its popup again.
  10. 3.1Feb 15, 2026

    A side panel that guides you

    • NewSide panel: opens from the DivPic icon and stays open while you pick.
    • NewOn-screen instructions for single and multi-select.
    • ImprovedClearer keyboard shortcut display.
    • FixedEscape now reliably cancels a selection.
  11. 3.0Jan 30, 2026

    Style your screenshots

    • NewPNG style: padding, corner radius, shadow and background, with a live preview. Your settings are kept.
    • NewCopy an SVG to the clipboard as code.
    • ImprovedThe shortcut hint matches your system: Cmd on a Mac, Ctrl elsewhere.
  12. 2.1Jan 29, 2026

    Sharper SVG text

    • ImprovedSVG text keeps its italics, letter and word spacing and underlines.
    • ImprovedHidden, clipped and covered elements are left out of SVG exports.
    • ImprovedImages in SVG keep the cropping they have on the page.
    • FixedHeadings no longer show their text twice in an SVG.
    • NewA "Contact Developer" link in the popup.
  13. 2.0Jan 27, 2026

    SVG export and multi-select

    • NewSVG export: save an element as an editable vector.
    • NewMulti-select: pick several elements and capture them together.
    • ImprovedSeveral elements in one SVG keep their positions from the page.
    • ImprovedA redesigned popup.
  14. 1.0Apr 3, 2025

    Screenshot any element

    • NewClick any element on a page to save it as a PNG.
    • NewCopy the screenshot to the clipboard instead of saving it.
    • NewA keyboard shortcut starts picking, and Escape stops it.
