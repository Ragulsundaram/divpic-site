How it works

Three steps. About five seconds.

1

Open the toolbar

Click the DivPic icon or press ⌘ ⇧ E (Ctrl ⇧ E on Windows). A small toolbar floats over the page.

2

Point and click

Hover to highlight any element, then click it. Or hit Full page to take everything in one go.

3

Save, copy or paste

Download a PNG or SVG, copy to the clipboard, or paste straight into a Figma file.

Figma mode

From live site to layers you can edit.

Stop rebuilding UI by hand from screenshots. DivPic reads the page's real structure and styles, so what lands in Figma is something you can work with.

Help

Questions, answered.

How do I set it up in Safari?
  1. Install DivPic from the Mac App Store and open the DivPic app once.
  2. In Safari, open Settings → Extensions and turn on DivPic.
  3. Allow DivPic on the websites you want to capture, or on all websites.
  4. Click the DivPic button in the Safari toolbar, or press ⌘ ⇧ E.
The DivPic button does nothing

Reload the page. Tabs that were open before DivPic was installed or turned on don't have it yet. On Safari, also check that DivPic is allowed on that site under Settings → Websites → DivPic.

Do I need an account?

No. Capturing, saving and copying all work without one. Only Figma mode asks you to sign in with Google.

Do I need a Figma plugin?

No. DivPic puts the capture on your clipboard. Open any Figma file and paste.

Can I capture a whole page, not just one element?

Yes. Pick Full page in the toolbar instead of selecting an element.

Are my screenshots uploaded anywhere?

No. They're made on your device and stay there. The privacy policy covers every permission DivPic asks for and why.

Something looks off in Figma. What now?

Some pages use effects Figma can't represent exactly. Send the page URL and a screenshot to ragulsundaram15@gmail.com and it'll get looked at.
