DivPic

Capture any part of a web page

Save it as a PNG or SVG, copy it to the clipboard, or paste it into Figma as editable frames. Available for Chrome, Firefox and Safari.

Getting started on Safari

  1. Install DivPic from the Mac App Store and open the DivPic app once.
  2. In Safari, open Settings → Extensions and turn on DivPic.
  3. Allow DivPic on the websites you want to capture (or on all websites).
  4. Click the DivPic button in the Safari toolbar, or press Shift-Command-E.

Common questions

The DivPic button does nothing

Reload the page. Pages that were open before DivPic was turned on don't have it yet. Also check that DivPic is allowed on that website in Safari Settings → Websites → DivPic.

Do I need an account?

No. Capturing, saving and copying work without one. Only Figma mode asks you to sign in with Google.

Are my screenshots uploaded?

No. Screenshots are made on your device and never leave it. See the privacy policy.
